A little help.

Understand the choices, permissions, and current limits of the reviewed Optidy TestFlight 1.0 (2) build.

Photos access and iCloud originals

Optidy needs read and write access to your photo library to review photos and save copies. Limited Photos access means it can only work with the items you allow. Change this in your iPhone’s Photos permission settings for Optidy. Library analysis uses locally available resources. If an original is in iCloud, Apple Photos may need to download it for a copy you request; check your connection and available device space.

Matching or similar: what is the difference?

Similar photos are suggestions to review, not a finding that one is unnecessary. Matching image data means the current image bytes match; motion, metadata, and other source details may still differ. Compare each item before deciding.

Creating a still from a Live Photo

A still does not keep the Live Photo’s motion or sound. Optidy creates and verifies a new photo before offering a separate choice about the original. Review the new photo yourself. If verification fails, keep the original and try again only after checking Photos access and free working space. Creating the copy does not remove the original.

Deletion and Recently Deleted

You choose and confirm any original removal. Apple Photos controls deletion, including Recently Deleted and iCloud Photos behavior. Optidy cannot empty Recently Deleted directly. Candidate sizes, copied bytes, and moved bytes do not prove that storage has been freed. Check iPhone Storage after completing your choices in Photos.

Understanding a storage screenshot

Choose a screenshot from iPhone Storage. Optidy reads its visible text on your device and gives guidance based on that image. It is not a live inventory and may miss or misread text. Optidy cannot inspect or clear another app’s private files or cache.

Smaller video copies in this test build

Optidy can prepare and verify a strictly smaller 1080p or 720p copy of one compatible standard SDR video, without upscaling. Saving the prepared copy to the Photos library still needs physical-device verification. The original is kept. HDR, slow-motion, Cinematic or edited, spatial, ProRes, compound, and already-small videos are outside this scope. A copy needs free working space and can change quality. No universal format support or guaranteed saving is promised.

Ads, consent, Plus, and purchases

TestFlight 1.0 (2) contains Google Mobile Ads SDK 12.14.0, User Messaging Platform SDK 3.1.0, and StoreKit, Plus, reward, and purchase code paths. Advertising is disabled because the required runtime flag is absent. No live in-app product or price is configured, and this site makes no purchase or subscription offer. These implementation paths do not establish an active ad request, completed purchase, entitlement, or tracking event.

Official contact is pending.

An official, monitored Optidy support and privacy contact has not been confirmed. This public site does not accept messages or file uploads. Once a channel is published, useful diagnostic details will be the Optidy version, iOS version, and a short issue description; do not send personal media unless support specifically requests it and you choose to do so.
