Optidy for iPhone and iPad

Support

Need help with Optidy? Email optidy@oda.so. You will find answers to common questions below.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Optidy, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with, and make sure you are online. If Plus still does not appear, email us with the date of your purchase. Please do not send payment details.

How do I cancel Optidy Plus?

In Optidy, go to Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Optidy. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Plus stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting Optidy does not cancel the subscription. See Apple’s guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. ODA cannot issue refunds.

What does Optidy delete?

Only the items you select and confirm. Optidy asks you first, and then iOS asks again. Before you remove a Live Photo, Optidy saves a still copy and checks it. A smaller video copy is saved as a new video, and Optidy does not delete the original video. Anything in Protected items is never deleted, and Favorites are left out of scans unless you turn on Include Favorites in scans. Optidy does not delete or change anything in the Files app.

Where do deleted items go?

They go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos. They stay there for 30 days, and you can recover them during that time. The space is freed only after they are removed from Recently Deleted. Optidy cannot empty this album. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions apply across all your devices.

How do I protect a photo from deletion?

Open the photo or video in Optidy and choose Never delete. It is added to Settings › Protected items, left out of future scans and never deleted by Optidy. You can remove protection there at any time.

How do I change Photos access?

Open the Settings app, go to Apps › Optidy › Photos, and choose the access you want. With limited access, Optidy can only see the items you allow.

How do I change ad and tracking choices?

If Google’s consent message applies where you live, go to Optidy Settings › Review ad privacy choices. To change tracking permission, open the Settings app and go to Privacy & Security › Tracking. Optidy Plus removes ads.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Optidy analyzes your library on your device and does not upload your photos or videos. Read more in the Privacy Policy.

Contact us

Email optidy@oda.so. Tell us your device, iOS version, Optidy version and what happened. You can find the Optidy version in Settings › About. Please do not send photos or videos unless we ask and you are comfortable sharing them.
