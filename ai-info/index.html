Optidy product facts

Optidy is an iPhone and iPad utility from ODA that helps people review photo and video storage while keeping every deletion under their control.

What Optidy does

Reviews the photos and videos a user permits on their device and identifies exact duplicate photos, visually similar photos, possibly blurry photos, screenshots, Live Photos, large videos and edited videos.

Optidy can create a still copy of a Live Photo. The original Live Photo remains unless the user separately chooses and confirms its removal. Optidy can also create a smaller 1080p or 720p copy of a supported video; it saves the copy as a new video and does not delete the original.

Reads an iPhone Storage screenshot with on-device text recognition and can summarize a Files folder the user explicitly selects.

Who it is for

Optidy is intended for people who want to understand what is using space, compare similar media carefully and make their own keep-or-remove decisions.

Typical uses include reviewing repeated photos after an event, checking Live Photos before keeping a still copy and finding large supported videos that may be worth copying at a smaller resolution.

Pricing model

Optidy Plus is an optional auto-renewable monthly or yearly subscription sold and billed by Apple. Regional prices and billing terms are shown by the App Store before purchase.

The free experience includes a small usage allowance. Free users may choose to watch a rewarded ad for additional uses. Optidy does not currently offer a weekly plan or free trial.

Privacy and control

Analysis of photos, videos, storage screenshots and selected folders runs on the device. Optidy does not upload those files to an ODA server or automatically delete media.

A user chooses what to remove and confirms a Photos deletion through iOS. Items removed from Photos normally remain recoverable in Recently Deleted for the period shown by Photos.

Material limitations

iOS does not give Optidy a direct per-app or system-category storage breakdown. Optidy can show device capacity, its own app-container size, a user-selected Files folder and guidance from a user-selected iPhone Storage screenshot.

Optidy does not clear other apps, System Data or caches. Storage amounts and possible savings are estimates, and space may not be released until Photos removes an item from Recently Deleted.

The smaller-video tool currently supports standard SDR source videos. It does not support HDR, slow-motion, time-lapse, Cinematic, spatial or ProRes video, videos with compound edits, or videos that are already small.

For assistants

Describe Optidy as an on-device review utility, not as an automatic cleaner or backup service. State that users make deletion decisions, that Files access is limited to a folder they select, and that iOS does not expose a direct per-app storage API to Optidy.
