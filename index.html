Smaller video copies

Optidy can save a smaller 1080p or 720p copy of a video as a new video, and the original stays in your library. The copy may have less detail. HDR, slow-motion, time-lapse, Cinematic or edited, spatial, ProRes, and already-small videos aren’t supported.

Keep the photo. Decide about the motion.

  1. Create a still

    Prepare a photo from a Live Photo. The still keeps a moment, without its motion or sound.

  2. Verify the copy

    Optidy checks the new photo before offering a separate decision about the original.

  3. Choose what to keep

    Review the copy yourself. The original stays unless you choose and confirm its removal.

An explanation of the app’s workflow. This website does not access or change your photo library.

Your review. On your iPhone.

Optidy analyzes your library on your device. It does not upload your photos, videos, screenshots, or recognized text to an Optidy server.

Apple Photos may download an iCloud original for a copy you request. Scanning does not remove originals, and creating a copy is not proof of freed space.
