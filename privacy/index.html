Public technical draft. Independent legal review pending.

This page reflects the reviewed Optidy TestFlight 1.0 (6) technical evidence. The legal operator, legal-notice address, monitored contact, and final legal approval remain pending. Do not treat it as a final policy or final terms.

Your library and permissions

Optidy requests Photo Library read and write access. You control the scope through iOS, including Limited Photos access. The app can review accessible media, create copies, and request deletion only through your explicit choices.

Photo and video content is used for thumbnails, local matching and similarity analysis, and preparation of still copies. The app reads local asset identifiers, filenames, dates, dimensions, media types and sizes, favorite and hidden states, and album membership. A still copy preserves available source location metadata; the current app does not request a separate Location permission.

Processing on your iPhone

Optidy does not upload your photos, videos, screenshots, or text recognition results to an Optidy server. Processing is performed by the app on your device. Library measurement and matching use locally available PhotoKit resources.

If an original is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple Photos may download it when needed for a copy operation you request. This is why on-device processing does not mean the app never needs an internet connection.

A screenshot you choose

The storage guide reads only the screenshot you select. Apple’s on-device Vision text recognition extracts visible app labels and sizes. The image and findings are held in app state for that view; the current app does not persist or upload that screenshot or its recognized text.

What remains on the device

Optidy stores an introduction-completed setting and a small local record of verified replacements in app preferences. Replacement records include local Photos identifiers, an image digest, dimensions, preparation date, and state. They keep copy preparation separate from deletion.

Library scan results are stored locally for up to seven days so the latest review can be reopened. The scan cache is discarded if the Photos permission scope changes, a referenced item is missing, or a referenced item has changed since the scan.

There is no fixed expiry or dedicated in-app erase control for the verified-replacement record in the reviewed implementation. It remains in local app storage unless the app’s data is removed. Photos created by the app remain in your Photos library.

For one compatible standard SDR video, the reviewed build can prepare and verify a smaller local copy before a separate save choice. Its service uses task-specific temporary files with cleanup on error, cancellation, or successful save. Physical-device saving to Photos remains unverified, and the original is not automatically replaced or deleted.

Accounts, ads, consent, and purchase code

The reviewed build has no Optidy account and no app-operated developer backend. Photos, videos, screenshots, recognized text, and local replacement records are not uploaded to an Optidy server. No account-deletion flow is available for an account that does not exist.

TestFlight 1.0 (6) embeds Google Mobile Ads SDK 12.14.0 and User Messaging Platform SDK 3.1.0. Advertising fails closed because the required OptidyAdsRuntimeEnabled flag is absent. The consent component is present at startup, but the reviewed evidence does not establish a production ad request or active ad delivery.

Google’s embedded SDK privacy manifests declare potential use of coarse location, product interaction, advertising data, crash data, performance data, other diagnostic data, and device identifiers. The declared purposes include third-party advertising, the developer’s advertising or marketing, analytics, and app functionality depending on the category. The SDK marks device identifiers as linked to the user and used for tracking. The exact app has no tracking-purpose string and no App Tracking Transparency request. Whether a device identifier is active in this configuration and the final tracking disclosure remain unresolved; this evidence neither asserts active tracking nor proves tracking is absent.

The build also contains StoreKit and Plus, reward, and purchase code paths. No live in-app product or price is configured, this site makes no commercial offer, and the reviewed app has no developer purchase server. Code presence does not establish a completed purchase, entitlement, subscription, or reward.

Your controls

You can change Photos permissions in iOS settings, remove the app’s local data, and manage media in Apple Photos. You decide whether to delete originals. Deletion and Recently Deleted are controlled by Apple Photos; Optidy cannot purge Recently Deleted or guarantee immediate storage recovery.

This website

This public website has no site analytics, advertising, forms, accounts, third-party embeds, or application cookies in the reviewed candidate. It does not request access to your photo library.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends technical request information, such as an IP address and browser request details, to the hosting provider.

Operator and privacy contact

The operator’s full legal identity, legal-notice address, and verified monitored privacy contact are pending owner confirmation. Requests cannot be submitted through this website. The final policy, effective date, applicable rights, and provider disclosure require independent legal review after those facts are approved.
