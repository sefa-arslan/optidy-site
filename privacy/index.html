Privacy, in plain language.

This draft describes the current app implementation and this website separately. It is not the final privacy policy.

Draft for review. Not a final policy.

The legal operator, legal-notice address, and verified Optidy contact are awaiting owner confirmation. These pages are not ready for public publication.

Your library and permissions

Optidy requests Photo Library read and write access. You control the scope through iOS, including Limited Photos access. The app can review accessible media, create copies, and request deletion only through your explicit choices.

Photo and video content is used for thumbnails, local matching and similarity analysis, and preparation of still copies. The app reads local asset identifiers, filenames, dates, dimensions, media types and sizes, favorite and hidden states, and album membership. A still copy preserves available source location metadata; the current app does not request a separate Location permission.

Processing on your iPhone

Optidy does not upload your photos, videos, screenshots, or text recognition results to an Optidy server. Processing is performed by the app on your device. Library measurement and matching use locally available PhotoKit resources.

If an original is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple Photos may download it when needed for a copy operation you request. This is why on-device processing does not mean the app never needs an internet connection.

A screenshot you choose

The storage guide reads only the screenshot you select. Apple’s on-device Vision text recognition extracts visible app labels and sizes. The image and findings are held in app state for that view; the current app does not persist or upload that screenshot or its recognized text.

What remains on the device

Optidy stores an introduction-completed setting and a small local record of verified replacements in app preferences. Replacement records include local Photos identifiers, an image digest, dimensions, preparation date, and state. They keep copy preparation separate from deletion.

There is no fixed expiry or dedicated in-app erase control for this record in the reviewed implementation. It remains in local app storage unless the app’s data is removed. Photos created by the app remain in your Photos library.

Video reduction is in development. Its service uses task-specific temporary files with cleanup on error, cancellation, or successful save. Its complete user-facing workflow is not available yet.

Accounts and other services

The current app has no Optidy account, developer backend, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, billing integration, or third-party telemetry SDK. No account-deletion flow is needed for an account that does not exist. Any later provider or business-model change will require a new review of these disclosures.

Your controls

You can change Photos permissions in iOS settings, remove the app’s local data, and manage media in Apple Photos. You decide whether to delete originals. Deletion and Recently Deleted are controlled by Apple Photos; Optidy cannot purge Recently Deleted or guarantee immediate storage recovery.

This website

This local preview has no analytics, advertising, forms, accounts, third-party embeds, or application cookies. It does not request access to your photo library.

GitHub Pages is the planned public host. Visiting a publicly hosted website sends technical request information, such as an IP address and browser request details, to the hosting provider. The final hosting disclosure and provider privacy reference must be confirmed at publication.

Operator and privacy contact

The operator’s full legal identity, legal-notice address, and verified privacy contact are pending owner confirmation. Requests cannot be submitted through this preview. The final policy, effective date, and applicable rights will be reviewed once those facts are approved.
