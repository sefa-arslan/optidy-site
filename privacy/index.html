Privacy Policy

Optidy is an app for iPhone and iPad developed by ODA. This policy explains what information Optidy uses, why it is used, where it is kept, who else receives it and the choices you have. It also covers this website.

Who is responsible

Optidy is developed and operated by ODA. ODA is responsible for the information in this policy that ODA itself handles. Apple and Google handle some information under their own privacy policies, as explained below.

Privacy questions and requests: optidy@oda.so.

No account and no Optidy server

You do not need an account to use Optidy. Optidy does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

ODA does not run a server that receives your photos, videos, scan results or settings, and Optidy contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with ODA in a form that does not identify you.

Your photos and videos

Optidy asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates, similar and blurry photos, Live Photos, screenshots, large videos and edited videos. You can give full or limited access, and change it at any time in iOS Settings.

All analysis runs on your device. Optidy uses the photos and videos themselves and the details Photos provides about them, such as the date, size, dimensions, media type and whether an item is a favorite. Optidy does not upload your photos or videos, does not send them to ODA, Google or anyone else, and does not use them for ads.

If an original is stored only in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it to your device when you play it or ask Optidy to copy it. Apple handles that download as part of iCloud Photos.

Changes Optidy makes to your library

Optidy changes your library only when you ask it to. It can:

  • save a still copy of a Live Photo. The copy keeps the location information of the original, if there is any;
  • save a smaller 1080p or 720p copy of a video;
  • save the edited version of a video as a new video, or, after you confirm, revert an edited video to its original;
  • delete the items you select, after you confirm in Optidy and again in the iOS prompt.

Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. Optidy cannot empty Recently Deleted. If you use iCloud Photos, Photos applies these changes across all your devices.

Items you add to Protected items are left out of every scan and are never deleted by Optidy.

Storage screenshots and folders you choose

If you choose a screenshot of iPhone Storage, Optidy reads its text on your device with Apple’s Vision framework to show which apps use the most space. The screenshot and its text are kept only while that screen is open. They are not saved or uploaded.

If you choose a folder in the Files app, Optidy reads the names, sizes, types and dates of the files in it to show what takes up space. Optidy only reads the folder. It does not change, move or delete files, and the results are not saved or uploaded.

On your device, Optidy also reads your device’s total and free storage and how much space Optidy itself uses.

Information saved on your device

Optidy saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your latest scan results, so you do not have to scan again. They are used for up to 7 days and are replaced when you scan again;
  • your Protected items list and whether Favorites are included in scans;
  • records of the copies Optidy has prepared, so it can check a copy before offering to delete the original;
  • which edited videos you have already reviewed;
  • your remaining free reviews and changes, and the expiry date of your Optidy Plus subscription, so the app can show your status right away;
  • whether you have finished the introduction.

Google’s consent tools also save your ad privacy choices on your device. ODA cannot see any of this information. It stays on your device until you change it in Optidy or delete the app. Like other app data, it can be included in your device backups.

When Optidy makes a smaller video copy, it uses temporary files and deletes them when the copy is saved or canceled, or if an error occurs.

Optidy Plus and purchases

Optidy Plus subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. ODA does not receive your payment card details, billing address or Apple Account details.

Optidy receives from Apple the product and transaction status it needs to unlock Plus and to restore it. This check happens on your device. Apple keeps purchase records under its own terms and privacy policy.

Ads, consent and tracking

If you do not have Optidy Plus, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to get more free reviews and changes. An ad loads only after you tap to watch one. Optidy shows ads with the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) and manages ad consent with Google’s User Messaging Platform.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent message appears when you open Optidy. In those regions you can change your choices at any time in Optidy Settings › Review ad privacy choices.

After the consent process finishes during first launch, Optidy may show the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether Optidy may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites. Optidy requests this permission only if your consent choices allow personalized ads and you have not already made a tracking choice. If iOS cannot show the prompt at that point, Optidy tries again the first time you tap Watch an ad, before requesting the ad. If your consent choices do not allow personalized ads, Optidy does not request tracking permission and requests only non-personalized ads. You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Optidy requests personalized ads only if you allow tracking and your consent choices allow personalized ads. In every other case, it requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads are not based on your past activity, but Google still processes some information to show and measure the ad and to prevent fraud.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may process your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance data. Apple gives apps the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Optidy Plus removes ads. While Plus is active, Optidy does not load ads.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to attach. We use them only to answer you.

Messages pass through our email service providers. We keep support messages only as long as we need them to help you and to handle any follow-up, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.

The website has no analytics, ads, cookies, forms or accounts, and it cannot access your photo library.

Legal bases

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies, ODA relies on these legal bases:

  • your consent, for personalized ads and tracking. You can withdraw it at any time in Optidy Settings or iOS Settings;
  • performance of our contract with you, to provide Optidy and Optidy Plus;
  • our legitimate interests, to answer support requests and keep Optidy working and secure;
  • compliance with legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Optidy does not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

International transfers

Apple, Google, GitHub and our email service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. They protect it under their own policies and the safeguards required by applicable law.

How long information is kept

  • Photos, videos, screenshots and folder contents: ODA never receives or stores them. Screenshot and folder results are discarded when you leave that screen.
  • Information saved on your device: until you change it in Optidy or delete the app. Scan results are used for up to 7 days.
  • Support emails: only as long as needed to help you, as described above.
  • Apple, Google and GitHub keep information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask whether we process your personal data, and to access, correct or delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy of it and withdraw your consent. Under Article 11 of KVKK, you may also learn the purpose of the processing and whether data is used for that purpose, learn which third parties in Türkiye or abroad receive it, ask that corrections or deletions be passed on to them, object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Most of the information Optidy uses never leaves your device, so you can delete it yourself in Optidy or by deleting the app. For anything ODA holds, such as support emails, write to optidy@oda.so. We reply within the time required by law; for requests under KVKK, within 30 days at the latest.

For ad data, you can also use Google’s privacy controls. You have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In Türkiye, this is the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu).

Children

Optidy is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. ODA does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your photos and the results of their analysis stay on your device, where iOS protects them. Optidy stores its scan results with iOS data protection, so they can be read only while your device is unlocked.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Privacy questions or requests: optidy@oda.so. Help with the app: Optidy Support.
