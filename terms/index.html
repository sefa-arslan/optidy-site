What the test build does

Optidy is an iPhone storage review utility. TestFlight 1.0 (6) helps you review similar or matching photos, prepare verified still copies of Live Photos, understand a storage screenshot you choose, and prepare and verify a smaller 1080p or 720p copy of one compatible standard SDR video. Physical-device saving of the video copy to Photos remains unverified. This site does not announce App Store availability or a release date.

Review before removing

You remain responsible for reviewing suggested matches and new copies before choosing to delete an original. Similarity is not a guarantee of equivalence. Matching current image bytes do not establish that all motion, metadata, or source resources are identical.

A still photo does not preserve Live Photo motion or sound. Creating or verifying a still is separate from deleting the original. It is not a backup of your photo library.

Storage and compatibility

Measured candidate sizes, output sizes, and bytes moved to Recently Deleted are not guaranteed freed space. Library scan results may remain locally available for up to seven days and are invalidated when Photos access or referenced media changes. Photos permissions, iCloud availability, working space, media formats, and Apple Photos behavior can affect an operation.

Optidy does not directly clear another app’s cache, purge Recently Deleted, or promise immediate savings, permanent recovery, lossless conversion, or support for every media format. Review a copy’s quality and contents before making a decision.

Ads, Plus, and purchase status

The test build contains Google Mobile Ads SDK 12.14.0, User Messaging Platform SDK 3.1.0, StoreKit, and Plus, reward, and purchase code paths. Advertising is disabled because the required runtime flag is absent. No live in-app product or price is configured, and this website makes no purchase, subscription, or advertising offer.

Internal testing status

Optidy 1.0 (6) is an internal TestFlight build. Its scope may change before public release. This site does not promise public availability, a release date, compatibility with every media format, or any commercial offering.
