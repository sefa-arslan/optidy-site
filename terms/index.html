What Optidy is being built to do

Optidy is an iPhone storage review utility. It helps you review similar or matching photos, prepare verified still copies of Live Photos, and understand a storage screenshot you choose. Smaller standard SDR video copies are in development. This site does not announce App Store availability or a release date.

Review before removing

You remain responsible for reviewing suggested matches and new copies before choosing to delete an original. Similarity is not a guarantee of equivalence. Matching current image bytes do not establish that all motion, metadata, or source resources are identical.

A still photo does not preserve Live Photo motion or sound. Creating or verifying a still is separate from deleting the original. It is not a backup of your photo library.

Storage and compatibility

Measured candidate sizes, output sizes, and bytes moved to Recently Deleted are not guaranteed freed space. Photos permissions, iCloud availability, working space, media formats, and Apple Photos behavior can affect an operation.

Optidy does not directly clear another app’s cache, purge Recently Deleted, or promise immediate savings, permanent recovery, lossless conversion, or support for every media format. Review a copy’s quality and contents before making a decision.

Development status

The app and its scope may change during development. This draft does not establish pricing, subscriptions, advertising, purchase entitlements, service availability, or refund terms. None is being offered by this website.
