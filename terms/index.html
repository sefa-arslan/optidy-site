What Optidy does

Optidy helps you understand and manage the storage used by your photos and videos. It finds duplicates, similar and blurry photos, Live Photos, screenshots, large videos and edited videos. It can make still copies of Live Photos and smaller copies of videos, and it can read a storage screenshot or a Files folder you choose. Features may change as Optidy is updated.

You decide what is deleted

Optidy deletes nothing on its own. You choose each item to delete and confirm it in Optidy and in the iOS prompt. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Check what you are removing before you delete it. “Similar” is a suggestion, not proof that two items are the same. A still copy of a Live Photo has no motion or sound, does not include captions you added in Photos, and is not added to shared albums the original is in. A smaller video copy has lower quality than the original.

Optidy is not a backup service. Keep a backup of anything you want to keep. Storage amounts shown in Optidy are estimates. The space you actually free depends on Photos, iCloud Photos and Recently Deleted.

Optidy Plus subscription

Optidy Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription, available as a monthly plan (1 month) and a yearly plan (1 year). With Plus, Optidy shows no ads and does not limit opening photo details or making changes.

The price is shown in Optidy before you buy, in your local currency, as set by the App Store for your country or region.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.

You can manage or cancel the subscription in Optidy under Settings › Manage subscription, or in your Apple Account subscription settings. If you cancel, Plus stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. Deleting Optidy does not cancel a subscription. See Apple’s guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple.

If the price of your subscription changes, Apple informs you in advance according to its rules.

Restoring purchases
